# Surface: Profit Toko landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Indonesian online seller with one or more Shopee / TikTok Shop stores who feels "ramai tapi uangnya nggak ada". Job: understand in seconds that Seller Center payout is not profit, believe Profit Toko can show the real number from files they already have, and act. Action: "Coba dengan data contoh" (opens the app with labelled sample data) or "Mulai dengan file tokomu" (opens the app on Upload). Returning visitors with saved data get "Lanjutkan ke dashboard". Free, no signup, no pricing on the page. Proof: the product itself on synthetic, labelled sample data ("Data contoh"); no testimonials, user counts or result claims. App moves to /app/; data stays per-browser (IndexedDB, same origin). Build path: code-led (no image generation; roll ran degraded, user locked card A).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The seller's own question, answered with their own paper. Refuses the SaaS hero (slogan, subcopy, button, row of three feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Nota Rangkap from the app: cool ground #E9EBF4, carbon #1E2A78 ink and result fields, ply colours as source codes (Order white, Income pink, Iklan yellow, HPP blue, Catatan lavender), perforated stub edges, Public Sans 800 with tabular numerals, loss red only on the loss figure.

STORY: Visitor recognises their situation in the headline numbers, sees the gap broken down by source file, then reads four seller questions (why payout is not profit, which store loses, when cash arrives, what to fix first), how to get the files, why the data is safe, FAQ, and acts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (brand left; Cara kerja, Pertanyaan, Buka aplikasi right). Headline at ~76px across two thirds: "Omzet Rp46 jt. Dana cair Rp34,7 jt. Untungnya minus Rp8 jt." with the loss in red; one-sentence subline; full-width perforated ply strip of six stubs ending in the carbon Profit real stub, labelled Data contoh; primary + secondary action and the trust line directly under the strip, all above the fold at 1440x900.

FORM: Question-led long page, position 7 of the grounded list; seed: seed:564bf6a50aff7fbf. Signature interaction: tapping a stub opens its source ledger (sample rows of that file, tinted in its ply) under the strip, like the app's trace; stubs deal in left to right once on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
